Lessons fix a swing. Coaching changes a game.

A lesson gives you a fix. Then you're on your own, and the old habit creeps back.

Coaching is different. We set a goal together, measure where you are, build the change over weeks, and test it on the course until it holds under pressure. You always know what you're working on and why. And you can see it working, because the numbers tell you.

How a coaching program works

  1. 1

    Assess

    We start with your goal, your game and your numbers. Video, launch monitor and force plate data show me where your strokes really go.

  2. 2

    Build

    We work on the two or three changes that matter most, with drills you can practice between sessions. Nothing gets added until the last change sticks.

  3. 3

    Take it to the course

    Playing sessions test the change with real lies, real targets and a real score. This is where most programs fall short, and where ours spend real time.

  4. 4

    Own it

    Sessions spread out as the change settles. We keep checking your numbers so a small slip never becomes a bad habit.

Is a coaching program right for you?

A program is a good fit if:

  • You have a clear goal, like a handicap, a scoring milestone or a tournament
  • You're willing to practice between sessions, even a little
  • You've taken lessons before and the changes didn't last
  • You want someone checking your progress, not just giving tips

If you just want a quick tune-up, a single private lesson might be all you need. We'll figure that out on our call.

What the technology tells me

FlightScope launch monitor. Club path, face angle, launch and spin at impact, so I can see the real cause of your miss.

Smart2Move force plates. How you use the ground. Power and consistency start from your feet.

OnForm video. Your swing in slow motion, side by side with earlier sessions, so you can see the change for yourself.
